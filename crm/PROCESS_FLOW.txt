CAMBODIA EDUCATION LEVELS:
──────────────────────────

Primary School (បឋមសិក្សា)          │  Grade 1 → Grade 2 → ... → Grade 6
                                     │               ↓
Lower Secondary (បឋមភូមិ)           │  Grade 7 → Grade 8 → Grade 9
                                     │               ↓
Upper Secondary (មធ្យមភូមិ)         │  Grade 10 → Grade 11 → Grade 12
                                     │               ↓
                                     │          🎓 GRADUATION


KEY TRANSITIONS (must be validated):
─────────────────────────────────────
Grade 6  →  Grade 7     (Primary to Lower Secondary)
Grade 9  →  Grade 10    (Lower Secondary to Upper Secondary)
Grade 12 →  Graduation  (No further promotion)
